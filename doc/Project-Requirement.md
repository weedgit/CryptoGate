# Project Requirement

We plan to launch a cryptocurrency payment platform project and would like your team to assist us in developing a systematic plan covering the business model, product design, technical architecture, compliance roadmap, licensing requirements, app store listing, and future token ecosystem.

## Preliminary project concept

Our preliminary project concept is divided into four phases.

### Phase 1: Merchant Payment Collection Platform

To provide cryptocurrency payment collection tools for hotels, travel agencies, transportation companies, retailers, and cross-border service providers. The platform will include features such as payment QR codes, payment links, order management, on-chain payment confirmation, merchant dashboards, and settlement reports. At the initial stage, the platform does not intend to handle client asset custody, conduct proprietary exchange activities, or operate any trading-matching services. The platform will only charge a technical service fee.

### Phase 2: Self-Developed Wallet

To research and develop an iOS / Android wallet product, and compare non-custodial wallet, fully custodial wallet, and MPC wallet solutions. Please focus on analyzing the legal responsibilities, regulatory boundaries, user asset security, and licensing requirements under different wallet models.

### Phase 3: Fiat Conversion and Settlement

In the future, we hope to support merchants in converting stablecoins such as USDT / USDC into USD, AED, or other fiat currencies. At the initial stage, conversion and settlement may be completed through third-party licensed institutions. Please assist us in designing a compliant cooperation model, fund flow structure, API integration model, and fee structure.

### Phase 4: Proprietary Token / Proprietary Blockchain Ecosystem

In the long term, we would like to explore the issuance of our own token or the development of a low-cost payment ecosystem similar to TRX. Please assist us in evaluating token issuance, white paper preparation, legal opinions, tokenomics, regulatory approvals, exchange listing, and the boundaries of marketing and promotion.

## Requested deliverables

We would like your team to focus on delivering the following:

- Overall business model design
- Product feature list and phased roadmap
- Compliance and legal boundary analysis
- Recommended jurisdiction of incorporation and licensing roadmap
- Assessment of whether the project involves regulatory categories such as VARA / VASP / Custody / Exchange / Transfer & Settlement / Payment Services
- Legal listing requirements for iOS and Android
- Cooperation model with third-party licensed conversion institutions
- Technical architecture and development budget
- Operating cost and revenue model
- Executable implementation plan for the Phase 1 MVP

## Core requirements

Our core requirements are that the project must be legal, eligible for app store listing, capable of opening bank accounts, able to integrate with licensed institutions, and scalable in the long term. At the same time, we wish to avoid inadvertently triggering high-regulatory-risk activities at the early stage, such as custody, exchange, trading platform operations, or security-token-related activities.
